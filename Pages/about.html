Tim Unverzagt

Level Design & Balancing

Hello, I’m Tim.

I’m a Level Designer & Balancer from Germany. Currently I am studying at the School for Games in Berlin. Besides focusing on level design & balancing, I have knowledge about feature design, production & programming. Because of my previous experiences within my semester projects at the School for Games.

I started playing all kinds of games since my early childhood. From this I noticed that I’m extremely interested in the process of how a game starts from idea to a finished product. Then over the years I started creating card games, then started playing DnD & after some time finally landed in my current apprenticeship.

My goal is to create games that inspire people the same way it did for me & to bring joy to those who play.
