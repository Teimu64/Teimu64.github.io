Hello, I’m Tim.

I’m a Level Designer & Balancer from Germany. I graduated from School for Games in Berlin in 2026. Besides focusing on level design & balancing, I have knowledge of feature design, production & programming. Because of my previous experiences with my semester projects at the School for Games.

I started playing all kinds of games from my early childhood. From this, I noticed that I’m extremely interested in the process of how a game starts from an idea to a finished product. Then, over the years, I started creating card games, then started playing DnD & after some time, finally landed in School for Games.

My goal is to create games that inspire people the same way it did for me & to bring joy to those who play.
